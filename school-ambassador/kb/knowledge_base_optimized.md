# Academic Curriculum

Category: school

## Kindergarten Curriculum

Questions this answers: What do they teach in Kindergarten? | What is the Nursery curriculum like? | How are early childhood classes taught?

The early childhood program for Nursery, LKG, and UKG utilizes a play-way and Montessori-inspired methodology. The curriculum focuses on sensory-motor development, social skills, and foundational phonics and numeracy in a nurturing environment.

## Primary School Curriculum

Questions this answers: What is the primary school curriculum? | How do students learn in grades 1 through 5?

The primary years for Grades 1 through 5 focus on building strong foundational literacy, numeracy, and environmental awareness. Oakridge employs an inquiry-based approach that encourages curiosity, hands-on learning, and cross-disciplinary exploration.

## Middle School Curriculum

Questions this answers: What subjects are in middle school? | How is middle school different?

Students in Grades 6 through 8 transition to a more structured syllabus where experiential learning is key. The middle school curriculum introduces specialized subject areas in the sciences and social sciences, focusing on critical thinking, collaborative projects, and digital literacy.

## Secondary School Curriculum

Questions this answers: How does the school prepare students for board exams? | What is the focus in grades 9 and 10?

Secondary School for Grades 9 and 10 offers rigorous preparation for international board examinations. The academic focus shifts to advanced analytical skills, specialized subject mastery, and independent research to equip students for higher education challenges.

## Senior Secondary Streams

Questions this answers: What streams are available in 11th grade? | Can my child study commerce? | What are the options for 12th grade?

Senior Secondary students in Grades 11 and 12 enter pre-university specialization tracks. Oakridge offers comprehensive stream options across Science, Commerce, Humanities, and Arts to cater to diverse career aspirations.

### Science Stream

The Science stream options include Medical with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology, and Non-Medical with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Computer Science.

### Commerce Stream

The Commerce stream focuses on comprehensive studies in Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, and offers a choice between Applied Mathematics or Informatics Practices.

### Humanities and Arts Streams

Humanities covers History, Political Science, Psychology, Sociology, and Literature. Arts offers specialized study in Fine Arts, Graphic Design, Performing Arts, and Media Studies.

## Language Program

Questions this answers: What languages do you teach? | Is Hindi mandatory? | Can my child learn French?

The primary medium of instruction across all grades at Oakridge is English. Hindi is taught as a mandatory subject to ensure regional linguistic proficiency. Starting from the primary level, students must also choose a second language, with offerings including Malayalam, Tamil, French, German, and Sanskrit.

## Homework and Assessment Policy

Questions this answers: Does the school give homework? | How are students graded? | What is the assessment system?

The school assigns grade-appropriate homework to reinforce classroom learning without overwhelming students. Assessments are continuous and comprehensive, blending formative tests throughout the term with summative examinations at the end of each term. Teachers focus on conceptual understanding rather than rote memorization.

## Learning Support and Special Education

Questions this answers: Do you help students with learning difficulties? | Is there an IEP program? | How do you support special needs?

The school maintains a dedicated Learning Support Department that creates Individualized Education Programs for students with mild to moderate learning difficulties. This program is guided by the wellness counselor to ensure every student receives appropriate academic and emotional support.

# Admissions Process

Category: school

## Admission Cycle and Age Criteria

Questions this answers: When do admissions open? | How old does my child need to be for Nursery? | What is the age cutoff for Grade 1?

The admission cycle for Oakridge opens on November 1st and closes on March 31st, with mid-term transfers depending on seat availability. For Nursery, the child must be 3 years old by March 31st. Kindergarten requires the child to be 4 or 5 years old. Grade 1 requires the student to be a minimum of 6 years old by March 31st.

## Application and Transfer Requirements

Questions this answers: What documents are needed for admission? | Do I need a transfer certificate? | What proof is required?

All admissions and transfers require an original Transfer Certificate from the previous school and official academic transcripts for the past two years. Parents must also provide government-issued ID proofs, verifiable local address proof, and general health check-up certificates including specific allergy disclosures.

## Admission Assessments by Grade

Questions this answers: Is there an entrance exam? | How do you evaluate students for admission? | Do parents need an interview?

Oakridge evaluates students differently based on their developmental stage and grade level to ensure academic readiness and school alignment.

### Kindergarten and Primary Admissions

Kindergarten admissions involve a brief, informal interaction session with the child and parents to understand developmental milestones. Primary School admissions require the previous year's academic reports and a basic interaction to evaluate school readiness.

### Middle and Secondary Admissions

Middle School requires the previous two years' report cards, a written placement assessment in core subjects, and a brief interview. Secondary School requires a rigorous entrance examination in core subjects and a behavior certificate from the previous institution.

### Senior Secondary Admissions

Stream allocation for Senior Secondary is strictly determined by Grade 10 final board examination results. Students and parents attend stream counseling sessions to align academic strengths with the appropriate subject combination.

## Campus Tours

Questions this answers: Can I visit the school before applying? | Do you offer campus tours? | How do I book a tour?

Prospective parents can schedule comprehensive campus tours before submitting an admission application. Tours are conducted by the admissions counselor every Tuesday and Thursday afternoon and must be booked in advance through the main school portal.

## Student Withdrawal Process

Questions this answers: How do I pull my child out of school? | What is the withdrawal process? | When do I get the transfer certificate?

Parents wishing to withdraw their child must submit a formal written notice to the administrative office at least 30 days in advance. The official Transfer Certificate will be issued only after all outstanding fee dues are cleared and library books are returned.

# Sports and Cultural Activities

Category: school

## Sports Infrastructure and Teams

Questions this answers: What sports facilities do you have? | Do you have a swimming pool? | Are there school sports teams?

The Oakridge campus boasts professional-grade athletic facilities including a synthetic turf football pitch, an athletic track, outdoor basketball courts, two indoor badminton courts, and a swimming pool supervised by certified lifeguards. All students are sorted into four houses for year-round inter-house sports tournaments. Professional coaches provide after-school training for school teams in football, basketball, athletics, and swimming.

## Cultural Events and Arts

Questions this answers: Do you have a school fest? | What arts programs are available? | Do students learn music?

Oakridge hosts an Annual Cultural Fest called OakFest, which features inter-house competitions in music, dance, theatrical drama, and literary arts. Students have regular access to comprehensive arts facilities including sound-proofed music rooms equipped with diverse instruments, a spacious dance studio, and dedicated art studios for painting, sculpture, and graphic design.

## The House Point System

Questions this answers: What are the school houses? | How does the house system work? | What do students win?

All students are assigned to one of four houses named Ignis, Terra, Aqua, or Ventus. The house point system rewards students for academic excellence, athletic achievements, and positive behavior. At the end of the academic year, the house with the highest total points is awarded the prestigious Principal Cup.

# Academic Calendar and School Timings

Category: school

## School Operating Hours

Questions this answers: What time does school start? | What are the school timings? | When do the gates close?

The school operating hours run from Monday through Friday. Grades 6 through 12 attend from 8:00 AM to 3:15 PM, and Kindergarten through Grade 5 attend from 8:00 AM to 2:30 PM. The main school gates close promptly at 8:00 AM every morning. Students arriving late must get a pass at the security desk.

## First Term Schedule and Breaks

Questions this answers: When does the school year start? | When is the summer break? | When are first term exams?

The first academic term commences in early April, marking the start of the new academic year. The summer break runs from May 15th to June 30th, with the school reopening on July 1st. Mid-term assessments are held in the third week of September, and the first term concludes in late September.

## Second Term Schedule and Final Exams

Questions this answers: When are the winter holidays? | When do final exams happen? | When is the Dussehra break?

The second academic term commences in early October. The calendar includes a one-week Dussehra break in mid-October and a winter vacation from December 24th to January 3rd. Annual board and final examinations occur from mid-February to mid-March, and the academic year officially concludes in late March.

## Parent Teacher Meetings

Questions this answers: When do PTMs happen? | How do I know when the parent teacher meeting is?

Parent-teacher meetings are conducted on Saturday mornings following the release of quarterly evaluation reports. Specific time slots are communicated to parents via SMS and the parent portal 10 days in advance of the meeting.

## Emergency Weather Closures

Questions this answers: How do I know if school is closed for snow? | What happens during heavy rain? | Do you notify us of closures?

In the event of severe weather emergencies like extreme rainfall or hazardous conditions, the school may declare a sudden closure. Parents receive immediate notification via the parent portal application and an automated SMS blast by 6:00 AM on the affected day.

# Fee Structure

Category: school

## Tuition Payment Schedule and Methods

Questions this answers: When are fees due? | How can I pay the school fees? | Can I pay fees in cash?

Tuition fees at Oakridge are structured across four quarterly installments. These installments are due on the 10th of April, July, October, and January. Payments are accepted via the online parent portal, demand drafts, or bank transfers. Cash transactions exceeding 5,000 rupees are not permitted on campus for security reasons.

## Fee Discounts and Scholarships

Questions this answers: Is there a sibling discount? | Do you offer scholarships? | How much is the concession?

Oakridge offers a 10 percent sibling concession on tuition for the younger sibling while both students are enrolled. Additionally, merit scholarships are available for Senior Secondary students in Grades 11 and 12 based on their previous board exam performance.

## Late Fee Penalties

Questions this answers: What happens if I pay fees late? | Is there a late fee fine? | Can my portal access be blocked?

Tuition payments received after the 10th of the installment month automatically incur a late fee penalty of 50 rupees per day. If fees remain unresolved for more than 30 days without prior approval, the school may temporarily suspend the student's access to the parent portal, transportation, and auxiliary services.

## Fee Refund Policy

Questions this answers: Can I get my money back if we leave? | Are fees refundable? | What is the refund policy?

Admission and registration fees are strictly non-refundable under any circumstances. If a student withdraws mid-term, the tuition fee for the current quarter is retained, but any advance payments made for subsequent quarters will be fully refunded to the original payment method.

Questions this answers: What does Weguide provide? | Who is the robot in the lobby? | What is Nova?

## Applied Robotics Curriculum

Questions this answers: Does Weguide teach classes? | Is there a robotics club? | Do students get AI kits?

Weguide provides applied robotics and artificial intelligence curriculum modules for the school. This includes project guides and workshop modules for middle and high school students during their designated STEM periods. ## Partnership Scope and Boundaries

Questions this answers: Does Weguide have access to student records? | Does Weguide collect fees? | Does Weguide decide grades?

Weguide covers the deployment, software updates, and educational support for the interactive AI kiosks and robotics hardware. Weguide is not involved in academic grading, admission decisions, or school fee collection, and their personnel do not possess access to confidential student records.

All Weguide kiosks and robotics operate strictly on local, anonymized data processing to ensure student privacy. The companion robot Nova does not record audio or video of students for external servers, complying with the school's strict data protection policies.
